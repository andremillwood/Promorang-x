## 1. Executive Overview & Two-Layer Paradigm

Promorang operates on a dual-layer engagement model that bridges 24/7 self-service utility with ritualized cultural entertainment.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       ALWAYS-ON LAYER (The 24/7 Utility)                    │
│   Marketplace • Discovery & Radar • Open Missions • Growth Hub • Referrals  │
└──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┘
                                       │ Synchronized with
┌──────────────────────────────────────▼──────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       OPS THEATRE (The Scheduled Show)                      │
│   Mon Kickoff • Tue Proof • Wed Freeze • Thu Drops • Fri Payout • Sat Gala  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Always-On Layer (The Utility / Economy)**:
   - 24/7 autonomous engagement: exploring Opportunity Radar, browsing marketplace inventory, asynchronous referrals, open missions, continuous Growth Hub staking, and secondary Piece trading.
2. **The Scheduled Layer ("Ops Theatre" / The Show)**:
   - The synchronized weekly rhythm and monthly crescendos that build cultural habit, urgency, visible scarcity (slot countdowns), public proof of payout, and community recognition.

### 1. Scenes & Venues
* **Scene**: The living community, subculture, or scene graph (e.g., Dancehall scene, Tech Hub, Foodie district).
* **Venue**: Physical location anchors tracked via OpenStreetMap inventory and verified on **Opportunity Radar**.

### 2. Moments & Sub-Moments
* **Moment**: A timely cultural container with a reason to gather (e.g., festival weekend, product launch, live show).
* **Sub-Moment**: Granular time-boxed chapters inside a Moment (e.g., *Soundcheck VIP*, *10 PM Mainstage Cypher*, *Secret Afterparty*). Enables high-urgency flash bounties.

### 3. Missions (Evolution of Tasks)
Objective-led action loops built on the doctrine: **`Notice -> Move -> Prove -> Unlock -> Grow -> Return`**.
* **Clip Missions**: 15s UGC video creation, remixes, and branded challenge prompts.
* **Check-In Missions**: Geo-verified physical presence at Radar hot spots.
* **Social & Referral Missions**: Content syndication, squad invites, and link sharing.
* **Survey & Poll Missions**: High-intent community voting and brand sentiment capture.

### 4. Content & Living Feed
* The raw fuel uploaded by creators, participants, and merchants.
* Powers the living scene feed, verifies mission proof, and generates promotional clip assets.

### 5. Pieces & The Vault
* **Pieces**: Digital participation artifacts and access passes unlocked through Moments and Missions. Retained in the user's personal **Vault**.
* Can be fractionalized, traded on the secondary AMM market, or staked for platform multipliers.

### 6. My Canon
* The permanent, verified cultural portfolio and social resume of an individual participant or creator. Records all completed missions, won bets, and collected Pieces.

## 3. The Discovery, Radar & Speculation Engine

### Opportunity Radar (Sensory Discovery)
* Scans real-world cities and scenes (Jamaica, Colombia, Ghana pilots) to track rhythm, density, and event surges.
* Automatically elevates surging venues into **Featured Moments** and deploys localized geo-bounties.

### Polls & Social Bets ("Content Interests")
* Gamified speculation layer where participants stake **Coins** on:
  * *Creator Bets*: Which creator's clip hits 5k views first.
  * *Event Bets*: Which performer/DJ wins the live clash.
  * *Brand Polls*: Community-voted product decisions funded by advertiser budgets.
* Winners split **Gems** prize pools; loses soft Coins.

## 4. The Canonical Currency & Growth Hub Architecture

### The Three-Currency Model

| Currency | Value Peg | Primary Source | Utility / Sink |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coins** | Soft In-Game Points | Free starter tasks, check-ins, daily quests, polls | Entering Social Bets, unlocking base perks, leveling up |
| **PromoShare Tickets** | Lottery Entry Units | Mission completions, referrals, streak bonuses | Entries into the Monthly PromoShare Live Jackpot |
| **Gems** | **1 Gem = 1 USD** | Paid campaigns, winning bets, external purchase | Marketplace purchases, Growth Hub staking, cashout |

### The Growth Hub (Unified Staking Vault)
Every participant, creator, merchant, and advertiser is a **Growth Hub User**:
1. **Boost Multipliers**: Lock Gems $\rightarrow$ earn $+20\%$ Coins on missions or get $15\%$ marketplace discounts.
2. **KickStarters**: Decentralized micro-funding for creator video shoots, merchant inventory, or advertiser pre-commitments.
3. **Channel Shields**: Staked insurance that protects PromoShare tickets during inactivity or preserves campaign slots during delays.

## 5. The Definitive Ops Theatre Schedule (The 7-Day & Monthly Rhythm)

```
 Monday          Tuesday         Wednesday       Thursday        Friday          Saturday        Sunday
┌──────────────┐┌──────────────┐┌──────────────┐┌──────────────┐┌──────────────┐┌──────────────┐┌──────────────┐
│ RADAR &      ││ PROOF &      ││ OPS DARK     ││ CAMPAIGNS &  ││ PAYOUTS &    ││ LIVE MOMENTS ││ DIGEST &     │
│ MISSIONS DROP││ LEADERBOARDS ││ ODDS FREEZE  ││ SUB-MOMENTS  ││ UGC REELS    ││ REDEMPTIONS  ││ VAULT TEASERS│
└──────────────┘└──────────────┘└──────────────┘└──────────────┘└──────────────┘└──────────────┘└──────────────┘
```

### Monday — Kickoff & Radar Drop
* **Discovery & Radar**: Releases this week's 3 Featured City Moments based on detected venue rhythms.
* **Missions**: 2–3 new weekly Missions drop into the **Today** view (Clip & Check-In Missions).
* **Polls & Bets**: Weekly speculation lines open (stake Coins on creators/events).
* **Comms**: Auto-scheduled weekly balance statements and teaser blasts.

### Tuesday — Verification & Social Leaderboard
* **Verification**: Ops & automated engines verify mission proofs and credit Coins + PromoShare tickets.
* **Poll Updates**: Mid-week betting odds movement published to Stories (`"Creator A leads with 64% backing!"`).
* **Leaderboard**: Top 5 earners and top-performing Moments spotlighted on social channels.

### Wednesday — Ops Dark & The Mid-Week Freeze
* **The Freeze**: Speculation lines and poll submissions lock. Odds freeze to build suspense.
* **Platform State**: No new ops drops; users engage in passive discovery and prepare for Thursday.

### Thursday — Campaign Window Launch & Sub-Moments
* **Scarcity & Slots**: Campaign Window A closes; Window B opens. Public slot counters update (`"3 of 10 slots left"`).
* **Sponsored Campaigns**: Advertisers launch paid campaigns with Gem prize pools.
* **Sub-Moment Scheduling**: Brands configure weekend time-boxed flash missions.

### Friday — Campaign Close, Gem Payouts & Proof Showcase
* **Payouts**: Campaign entries and social bet outcomes resolved $\rightarrow$ **Gems** disbursed from activation escrow to wallets.
* **UGC Showcase**: High-production UGC reels compiling the week's best creator clips published across channels.
* **Weekend Radar**: Map heatmap of weekend Moments published.

### Saturday — Live Moments, Marketplace & Piece Drops
* **Real-World Execution**: Live Moments activate in physical venues; attendance tracked via QR ticket scans and Radar.
* **Vault Piece Unlocks**: Attendees unlock limited **Pieces** into their Vault.
* **Marketplace Fulfillment**: Gem redemptions reconciled with merchant partners; unboxings spotlighted.
* **Growth Hub**: Weekly Gem staking commitments processed for upcoming multipliers.

### Sunday — Ops Dark, Automated Balance & Vault Digest
* **Automated Digest**: Users receive personalized summaries of moments attended, pieces unlocked, earnings, and next week's jackpots.
* **Creator of the Week**: Automated spotlight on top-performing creators.

### Month-End — PromoShare Live Gala
* **The Live Draw**: High-energy live stream draw for PromoShare ticket holders.
* **Moment of the Month**: Recognition and Gem grants awarded to the top creator, venue, and merchant.
* **Piece Evolution**: Multi-piece collectors level up their permanent Growth Hub tiers.

## 7. The Cold Start Bootstrap Engine

To resolve the two-sided marketplace chicken-and-egg dilemma without reliance on initial paid advertiser spend, Promorang deploys five architectural bootstrap mechanisms:

1. **Pre-Populated Opportunity Radar (OSM Ingestion)**: Ingests municipal OpenStreetMap data across target pilot territories (Jamaica, Colombia, Ghana) ensuring live maps and scenes are never blank on Day 1.
2. **Monthly Sampling Provisions (`merchant_sampling_allowance`)**: Gives every new merchant zero-cost free activation slots. Merchants supply simple vouchers/perks while Promorang generates foot traffic and UGC.
3. **Promorang-as-Host (`co_branded_activations`)**: When third-party ad spend is low, Promorang co-funds activations and seeds baseline Gem prize pools to maintain weekly habit.
4. **Territory Representative System (`representatives`)**: Local field ambassadors earn 10% commission on first-time venue onboarding and 2% recurring volume commissions.
5. **Founding Member Mechanics (`founding_members`)**: Wave 1 early adopters receive permanent lifetime Boost Multipliers, fee waivers, and elite Vault badges.

## 8. Stakeholder Onboarding Blueprint

Every persona follows a 3-step continuum: **`Step 1: First Move (60s)` $\rightarrow$ `Step 2: Rhythm Anchor` $\rightarrow$ `Step 3: First Reward Loop`**:

* **Participants**: Land on `Today` $\rightarrow$ complete 1-tap Starter Mission (Follow + Poll Vote) $\rightarrow$ receive +10 Coins & 1 Ticket $\rightarrow$ alerted that Gem winners cash out on Friday.
* **Creators**: Submit 1 video link $\rightarrow$ receive 50+ community task engagements within 24h $\rightarrow$ upsell to Thursday Boost Slots.
* **Merchants**: Claim Free Monthly Sampling Allowance $\rightarrow$ venue badged on Radar $\rightarrow$ scan first attendee voucher on Saturday with web-based `TicketScanner.tsx`.
* **Advertisers**: View public slot counter (`3 of 10 slots left`) $\rightarrow$ launch campaign in Thursday Window $\rightarrow$ receive Friday UGC asset reel & ROI analytics.

## 10. Supporting Integrity & Backend Systems

1. **PromoPush Layer**: Real-time push, SMS, and webhook triggers for flash bounties and localized Radar alerts.
2. **Multi-Asset Piece Market & AMMs**: Liquidity pools for trading fractional moment pieces with creator royalty splits.
3. **Multi-Tier Referral Matrix**: Viral squad structures with commission overrides on mission rewards and marketplace spend.
4. **Anti-Fraud & Verification Engine**: GPS geo-fencing, perceptual image hash checks, velocity limiters, and KYC tiers.
5. **Key-Gating Infrastructure**: Rule-based access control for VIP Sub-Moments, exclusive channels, and premium drops.
