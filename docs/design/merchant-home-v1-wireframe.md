# Merchant Home v1 — Functional Wireframe

## Header

**[Business Name]**

Supporting controls:
- Public storefront
- Business settings
- Current location/business context when multiple locations exist

## Primary action block

### What would you like to do?

Four high-priority actions:

1. **Get more customers**
   - Opens Promotion Builder with acquisition defaults

2. **Bring customers back**
   - Opens Promotion Builder with repeat-visit defaults

3. **Promote something**
   - Opens Promotion Builder with product/service promotion defaults

4. **Fill a slow period**
   - Opens Promotion Builder with time-window/local redemption defaults

Secondary text link: **See more goals**

## Results strip

Display four cards:

- **Customers brought in**
- **Attributed sales**
- **Returning customers**
- **Active promotions**

Do not show Gems, Points, Keys or PromoShare here.

If attribution is not yet available, explicitly label the metric as unavailable rather than fabricate or substitute vanity metrics.

## Recommended next move

One state-derived recommendation.

Priority examples:

### No business configured
**Finish setting up your business**
Add your location and basic business details before publishing a promotion.

CTA: **Finish setup**

### Business configured, no promotions
**Launch your first promotion**
Choose the business result you want and Promorang will configure the rest.

CTA: **Create promotion**

### Active promotion, no redemptions
**Your promotion is live. Get it in front of customers.**
Share the promotion or use its QR at your business.

CTA: **Share promotion**

### First redemption achieved
**You brought in a customer. Build on it.**
Create a return promotion for people who have already visited.

CTA: **Bring customers back**

## Live promotions

Each card/row should show:

- Promotion title
- Customer value
- Status
- Schedule / end date
- Redemptions
- Attributed sales when available
- Primary next action

Actions may include:

- View results
- Share
- Edit
- Pause
- Duplicate

## Recent customer activity

Plain-language activity examples:

- 3 customers redeemed **20% Off Lunch** today
- 1 customer returned after a previous promotion
- 8 people claimed your current promotion
- Your promotion generated JMD $12,500 in attributed sales this week

Do not expose event names such as `proof_verified`, `trigger_event`, or `offer_issuance`.

## Empty state

For a new merchant:

**Turn an offer into measurable customers.**

Choose what you want the business to achieve. Promorang handles the promotion setup and gives you a way to measure what happens.

CTA: **Create your first promotion**

Secondary CTA: **Set up my business**

## Mobile priority

Above the first scroll on mobile:

1. Business identity
2. Recommended next move
3. Primary outcome actions
4. At least two results metrics

Do not make the merchant scroll through education, branded economy explanations, or ROI simulations before reaching the operating interface.
