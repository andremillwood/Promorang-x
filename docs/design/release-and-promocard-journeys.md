# Releases and PromoCard journeys

Promorang stays a demand layer for places and communities. This cut makes two adjacent jobs honest.

## PromoCard journeys

The card is one object. The next verb depends on how the benefit was funded:

- **Place** — show a code or QR; merchant records use
- **Code** — copy a digital credential
- **Ship** — address, tracking, delivered
- **Credit** — claim puts value on the card
- **Handoff** — issuer confirms

`/stock` asks “how do they receive it?” before the perk kind. Brands are not forced through a counter scan.

## Releases

A Release is origin: song, news, episode, or video. The original stays on Spotify, the paper, YouTube. Promorang holds the catch: a room, a perk, a claim.

- Consumer Today and Discover show one live Release signal, not a Media tab next to Eat
- Opening the original starts the loop
- Sharing, reposting, and commenting do not pay
- Creators land on `/content-drops`, not missions
- `/watch-unlock` redirects to Releases; `/missions` remains for funded gigs
