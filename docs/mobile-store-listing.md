# Mobile store listing

Use this as the source of truth for the first store records. Marketing may refine the copy without making earnings, investment, guaranteed-reward, or cash-equivalence claims.

## Shared listing copy

**App name:** Promorang

**Subtitle / short description:** Discover Moments. Show up. Keep what follows.

**Full description:**

Promorang helps you discover local Moments, join experiences, check in, contribute content, and keep a private record of what happened. Follow the Scenes and communities you care about, receive useful updates, and see eligible access, rewards, receipts, and recognition in your Vault.

Hosts, creators, merchants, and brand partners can coordinate real-world participation with clear roles and outcome records. Availability of Moments, commerce, rewards, and partner tools varies by location and account role.

Promorang includes tools to report content, block users, manage notification preferences, and request account deletion.

**Keywords:** local events, experiences, community, creators, rewards, check in, merchants

**Category:** Lifestyle (primary); Social Networking or Events may be evaluated as the secondary category.

**Support URL:** `https://promorang.co/contact`

**Privacy URL:** `https://promorang.co/privacy`

**Account deletion URL:** `https://promorang.co/account-deletion`

**Support email:** `support@promorang.co`

## Reviewer notes template

- Provide a dedicated reviewer account with representative participant access and no production customer data.
- Explain where camera/QR scanning appears and provide a test QR or deterministic test path.
- Explain that user content can be reported and users can be blocked from the relevant content/profile menu.
- Point reviewers to Settings for account deletion and notification controls.
- State that native card checkout is limited to physical goods and real-world services/access. Digital products, purchased Gems, paid digital features, and sponsor funding are unavailable for purchase in the version submitted.
- Give exact steps for any role-specific host, creator, merchant, or brand screen included in review.
