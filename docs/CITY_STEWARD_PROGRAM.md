# Promorang City Steward Program

## Purpose

City Stewards are trusted local operators who help Promorang enter a city with credible supply, accurate context, and community accountability. The role exists to prevent empty city launches and extractive expansion.

## Founding cohort

Each launch city begins with 1 lead Steward and up to 4 contributing Stewards for a 90-day pilot. Stewards are selected before public city acquisition begins.

## Responsibilities

- Map the city's active Scenes, recurring Moments, venues, merchants, and cultural rhythms.
- Verify submitted places and public information without selling placement or ranking.
- Recruit an initial network of credible hosts, creators, merchants, and community leaders.
- Review cultural accuracy, safety concerns, and locally sensitive terminology.
- Coordinate the first 25 launch Moments and at least one funded local activation.
- Publish a short weekly city signal covering what opened, what happened, and what needs attention.

## Selection requirements

- Lives in or has sustained, current involvement in the city.
- Provides two credible local references.
- Can perform weekly verification and partner-support work.
- Discloses business, political, venue, promoter, and brand conflicts.
- Accepts privacy, safety, anti-bribery, and no-pay-to-rank rules.

## Access and controls

Steward access is role-based, logged, and limited to an assigned city. A Steward cannot approve their own listing, campaign, reward, or conflict-linked venue. Material moderation and money movement require a second approver. Promorang may pause access while reviewing accuracy, safety, manipulation, or conflicts.

## Cohort milestones

### Days 1–30: Map

- 3 anchor Scenes
- 10 founding creators or hosts
- 10 venues or merchants
- a verified city calendar and category map

### Days 31–60: Activate

- 10–15 live Moments
- first participant feedback sessions
- one locally relevant sponsor or partner brief

### Days 61–90: Prove

- 25 cumulative Moments
- 500 founding participants or a city-adjusted target
- repeat activity from hosts and participants
- city trust, fraud, and content-quality review

## Success measures

- Active Moments per week
- Verified participation rate
- 30-day participant return rate
- Repeat host and venue rate
- Listing correction and rejection rate
- Sponsor renewal or second activation intent
- Participant reports, safety incidents, and unresolved moderation time

## Recognition and compensation

Founding Stewards receive visible verified standing, training, local operating access, and documented contribution records. Any stipend, campaign fee, or performance reward must be defined in a written country-specific agreement and funded independently of listing approval or ranking. The role never guarantees income, equity, Pieces, or withdrawal rights.

## Graduation

At 90 days, a Steward may graduate to an ongoing city operator role, continue as a contributing Steward, or leave the cohort. Graduation depends on accuracy, trust, partner retention, participant return, and operational consistency—not raw recruitment volume.
